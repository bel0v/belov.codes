### 🐣 Daniel

Daniel turned 1 today. What a year! Older folks say he’ll turn 18 in the blink of an eye. That’s too fast! He’s a sweet and cheerful kid. How can your heart not melt when he comes in for a hug, and how can you be angry at him for not eating his breakfast, when he holds the spoon out to feed you first? He seems much more outgoing than his parents. Always very friendly with the other children at the playground, almost never shy at all. We’ve really bonded over this year. I love this little fella.

### 💫 Life and everything

Trying to reflect on where I am at this age of LLMs. Like many others around me, I feel this identity crisis. The joy of programming has been taken out of our trade, and all we’re left with are necessary chores. So the joy is yet to be found again — perhaps actually designing LLMs rather than using them (and become the horseman of Doom myself lol)? Focus on the product and try to ship something of my own? Then again, everything gets <a href="https://github.com/storytold/">reverse-engineered</a>, so in a few years would anyone even buy any programs at all?

Maybe do pet project programming by hand — just for the sheer process and joy of it, and become what calligraphers have become? Or perhaps walk away from it all and do some nature conservation projects? I don’t know yet.
